---
title: "Better Speakers in 3 Minutes"
description: Optimize your speaker's frequency response using measurements from spinorama.org.
---

import { Steps, Aside } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

<Steps>

1. **Open the Spinorama EQ screen**

   - **TUI**: Navigate to Configure > Spinorama EQ.
   - **Desktop**: Click the Spinorama tab.

2. **Search for your speaker**

   Type your speaker model (e.g., "KEF R3") and select it. SotF fetches real measurement data from spinorama.org.

3. **Click Optimize**

   The optimizer finds the best parametric EQ filters to flatten your speaker's response. Watch the loss curve converge.

4. **Apply the filters**

   Click **Apply** to load the correction EQ into your plugin chain.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Over 800 speakers are available in the spinorama.org database. If yours isn't listed, you can use the Room EQ workflow with your own measurements instead. See the [Speaker EQ guide](/guides/speaker-eq/) for details.
</Aside>
